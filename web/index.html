Step 3: Review & Sort

Look through each group of duplicates side by side. For each group, you can keep everything, mark the duplicates for removal, or delete them. Click any image to zoom in. Use keyboard shortcuts for speed: S=keep, M=mark as duplicate, D=delete, arrows=next/previous.
