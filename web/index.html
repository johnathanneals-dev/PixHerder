Step 2: Configure & Scan

Now choose how to look for duplicates. Exact finds identical copies (fast and accurate). Perceptual finds images that look similar even if they've been resized or edited. Start with Exact, then try Perceptual to catch more.
5
How closely images must match. 0 = nearly identical, 5 = similar (default), 10+ = loosely similar (may flag false matches).
Lower = stricter matching, fewer results, faster. Higher = looser matching, more results, slower.
Exact (MD5) finds byte-for-byte identical files. Fast, zero false positives. Start here.
Perceptual finds visually similar images even if resized or recompressed. Slower, may need review.

Scan Modes
Exact finds identical copies of the same file. Fast and accurate -- start here.

Threshold Guide (perceptual only)
0-2 Very strict -- only near-identical images
3-5 Recommended -- catches resized copies and minor edits
6-10 Loose -- may flag images that just look similar
11+ Very loose -- expect some false matches, review carefully

Step 3: Review & Sort

Look through each group of duplicates side by side. For each group, you can keep everything, mark the duplicates for removal, or delete them. Click any image to zoom in. Use keyboard shortcuts for speed: S=keep, M=mark as duplicate, D=recycle, arrows=next/previous.

Step 4: Explore & Finalize

Take a look at your cleaned-up files and the duplicates that were removed. When you're happy with the results, click Finished to wrap everything up.
